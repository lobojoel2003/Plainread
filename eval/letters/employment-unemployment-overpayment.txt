This notice is to inform you that you have been overpaid unemployment insurance
benefits. Our records show that you received benefits for the weeks ending 05/09/2026
through 06/13/2026 while you were employed full time by Copperfield Distribution Co. and
were earning wages that were not reported on your weekly certifications. Under Section
17-304 of the Westmara Unemployment Insurance Law, you were not eligible for benefits
for those weeks.

     Amount already recovered by offset:        $364.00
     BALANCE DUE:                             $1,820.00

This overpayment has been classified as non-fraud. No penalty has been assessed.

WHAT YOU MUST DO
Unless you file a timely appeal or are granted a waiver, you must repay the balance due
of $1,820.00. You may pay the full amount online at www.westmarajobs.example/repay, or
you may set up a repayment agreement by calling (608) 555-0190. Monthly payments of at
least $50.00 are generally accepted.

IF YOU DO NOT REPAY
If you do not repay the overpayment or enter into a repayment agreement, the Department
may deduct the balance from any future unemployment benefits, intercept your state
income tax refund, and refer the debt for collection. Interest may also be charged on
unpaid balances as permitted by law.

REQUEST FOR WAIVER
You may ask the Department to waive (forgive) all or part of this overpayment if the
overpayment was not caused by your fault and repaying it would cause you financial
hardship. To request a waiver, complete the enclosed Request for Waiver of Overpayment
and return it within 30 days of the mailing date of this notice.

YOUR RIGHT TO APPEAL
If you disagree with this determination, you have the right to appeal. Your appeal must
be filed within 30 days of the mailing date shown above. You may file an appeal online
at www.westmarajobs.example/appeal, by fax to (608) 555-0192, or by mail to the address
above. Late appeals may be dismissed unless you show good cause for the delay.

You may be represented at an appeal hearing by an attorney or another person of your
choice. Free legal help may be available from the Westmara Workers' Rights Clinic at
(608) 555-0193. Interpreter services are provided free of charge for all appeal
hearings.
