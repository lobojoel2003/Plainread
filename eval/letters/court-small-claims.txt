                  SMALL CLAIMS SUMMONS AND NOTICE OF HEARING

NOTICE TO DEFENDANT: YOU ARE BEING SUED. The Plaintiff named above has filed a Statement
of Claim against you in this Court. The Court may decide against you without hearing
your side unless you respond within the time stated below. Read all of the information
in this summons carefully.

1. The Plaintiff claims that you owe $3,475.00 for vehicle repair services alleged to
   have been performed on or about November 12, 2025, together with the filing fee
   of $75.00 and service costs of $48.50 paid by the Plaintiff.

2. A hearing on this claim has been set for Tuesday, April 14, 2026, at 8:30 a.m., in
   Courtroom 2B of the Lakemont County Courthouse, 400 West Juniper Street, Lakemont,
   Westmara. You must appear in person at the time and place stated above, even if you
   have filed a written Answer.

3. You must file a written Answer with the Clerk of the Small Claims Division
   no later than April 3, 2026, stating whether you agree or disagree with the claim and
   the reasons why. A copy of your Answer must also be mailed to the Plaintiff at the
   address shown on the Statement of Claim.

4. Bring to the hearing all witnesses, receipts, estimates, photographs, contracts, text
   messages and other evidence that support your side of the case. Documents that are
   not presented at the hearing may not be considered by the Court.

5. IF YOU FAIL TO APPEAR AT THE HEARING, A DEFAULT JUDGMENT MAY BE ENTERED AGAINST YOU
   FOR THE FULL AMOUNT CLAIMED, PLUS COSTS, AND YOUR WAGES, MONEY OR PROPERTY MAY
   THEREAFTER BE TAKEN WITHOUT FURTHER WARNING FROM THE COURT.

6. If you believe the Plaintiff owes you money arising out of the same transaction, you
   may file a counterclaim with your Answer. A counterclaim filing fee of $35.00 must be
   paid at the time of filing unless the fee is waived.

7. If you cannot afford to pay court fees, you may ask the Court to waive them by
   submitting a Request to Waive Court Fees, which is available from the Clerk or on the
   Court's website.

LANGUAGE ACCESS: If you do not speak or understand English well, you have the right to a
court interpreter at no cost to you. Ask the Clerk for an interpreter when you file your
Answer.

ACCESSIBILITY: Persons with disabilities who need an accommodation to participate in
court proceedings should contact the ADA Coordinator at (312) 555-0144 or
ada@lakemontcourts.example.

Free self-help information about small claims, including sample Answer forms, is
available at www.lakemontcourts.example/selfhelp or at the Self-Help Center, Room 108,
Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
