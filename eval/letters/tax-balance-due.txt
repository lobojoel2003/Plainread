including filing a certificate of tax lien, garnishing your wages, or levying your bank
account.

IF YOU DISAGREE
If you believe this notice is incorrect, you have the right to file a written protest.
To be timely, your protest must be filed within 60 days of the date of this notice, that
is, no later than May 22, 2026. Include a copy of this notice and any wage statements or
other records that support your position.

If you are experiencing a financial hardship, the Office of the Taxpayer Rights Advocate
may be able to help. Call (919) 555-0112.
