RE: NOTICE OF ADVERSE BENEFIT DETERMINATION - CLAIM DENIED

Dear Oluwaseun B. Hartigan:

Silverbirch Health Plan (the "Plan") has completed its review of the claim submitted by
the provider named above for services you received on March 30, 2026. Following that
review, the Plan has determined that the claim is denied in full.

Reason for denial: Under Section 4.3 (Outpatient Advanced Imaging) of your Evidence of
Coverage, MRI services require prior authorization, and no prior authorization request
was received by the Plan before the date of service. In addition, the clinical
information submitted by your provider did not document that conservative treatment,
such as at least six weeks of physical therapy, was attempted before the imaging was
ordered.

Because this claim was denied, you may be responsible for the full amount
billed, $4,860.00. If your provider is in the Plan's network, the provider may not bill
you for services denied for lack of prior authorization; please contact Member Services
if you receive a bill.

YOUR RIGHT TO APPEAL
You have the right to request an internal appeal of this decision. Your appeal must be
received by the Plan within 180 days of the date of this notice. You may submit your
appeal in writing to the address above, by fax to (614) 555-0165, by email to
appeals@silverbirchhealth.example, or by calling Member Services. Please include your
Member ID, the Claim Number, a copy of this notice, and any medical records or a letter
from your doctor that supports your request. You may also ask for, free of charge,
copies of all documents, records and other information relevant to your claim, including
the clinical criteria used to make this decision.

URGENT SITUATIONS: If your doctor believes that waiting for a standard appeal decision
could seriously jeopardize your life or health, you or your doctor may request an
expedited appeal by calling 1-800-555-0163. Expedited appeals are decided within 72
hours.

EXTERNAL REVIEW: If the Plan upholds its decision after your internal appeal, you may
have the right to request an external review by an Independent Review Organization that
is not part of the Plan.

FREE HELP: The Westmara Office of Health Insurance Consumer Assistance can help you file
your appeal at no cost. Call (614) 555-0166 or visit www.westmarahealthhelp.example.
