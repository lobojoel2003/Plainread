Re: Change in Your Monthly Retirement Benefit

Dear Mr. Brathwaite:

We are writing to tell you about a change in the amount of your monthly service
retirement benefit. Your benefit began on 1 March 2019, and it was last adjusted for the
annual cost-of-living increase on 1 January 2026.

WHY YOUR BENEFIT IS CHANGING
During a routine audit of member records, we found that 1.75 years of service credit for
your employment with the Fairhaven Public Library from 2003 to 2005 had been counted
twice. We have recalculated your benefit using 21.25 years of service credit instead of
23.00 years. All other information used to calculate your benefit, including your date
of birth (4 November 1961) and your final average salary, is unchanged.

YOUR NEW BENEFIT AMOUNT
Beginning with your payment for September 2026, which will be deposited on
1 September 2026, your benefit will be as follows:

                                       Current Amount      New Amount
     Gross monthly benefit               $1,945.10          $1,797.11
     Health insurance premium              $212.00            $212.00
     Federal income tax withheld            $96.00             $84.00
     Net monthly deposit                 $1,637.10          $1,501.11

We will not ask you to repay the higher amounts you received before this change.

TAX WITHHOLDING
Your federal income tax withholding has been adjusted automatically based on your new
gross benefit. If you want a different amount withheld, you must return the enclosed Tax
Withholding Election form by 31 July 2026.

IF YOU AGREE WITH THIS CHANGE, you do not need to do anything.

IF YOU DISAGREE WITH THIS CHANGE
You have the right to ask us to reconsider this decision. You must make your request in
writing within 60 days after you receive this letter. Send your request to the address
above or email it to benefits@wpers.example, and include your Member Number and any
records that show your service credit is incorrect.

If you ask for reconsideration within 10 days after you receive this letter, we will
continue to pay your current benefit amount until we make a decision. If our decision
does not change, you may have to pay back the difference between your current and new
benefit for the months you were paid the higher amount.

For more information about reconsiderations and appeals, visit www.wpers.example/appeals
or call Member Services.
