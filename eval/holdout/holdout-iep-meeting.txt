YOUR RIGHTS AS A PARENT
  * You are an equal member of your child's IEP team, and you have the right to participate in every decision about your child's special education services.
  * You have the right to bring other people who have knowledge or special expertise about your child, such as an advocate, to the meeting.
  * If you need an interpreter, one will be provided free of charge; please write your preferred language on the response form.
  * You may ask for a copy of the draft IEP and the assessment reports before the meeting.
  * You may audio-record the meeting if you tell the district at least 24 hours in advance.
  * Free help is available from the Hollowmere Parent Training and Information Center at 1-866-555-0148.

A copy of the Procedural Safeguards Notice is enclosed. It explains your rights in detail, including your right to disagree with the IEP and to request mediation or a due process hearing. You can also read it online at hollowmereusd.example/parentrights.
