WHY ARE WE SENDING THIS?
The law requires insurance companies to tell their customers, once a year, how they collect, use, share, and protect personal information. This notice describes the practices of all of the Thistlewood Mutual companies listed above.

WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?
Depending on the products you have with us, the personal information we collect may include:
   - your name, address, date of birth, and government identification numbers
   - your driving record, claims history, and payment history
   - medical information, when it is needed to handle a life or health-related claim
   - information from consumer reports, such as credit-based insurance scores, where allowed by law

HOW DO WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION?
We use physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards that comply with the law, including secured files and buildings, encryption, and limits on which employees can see your information. Our employees are trained on the importance of keeping your information confidential. We keep records for as long as the law requires, which is generally at least seven years after your policy ends.

YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS
You have the right to see the personal information we keep about you and to ask us to correct anything that is wrong. If we disagree with a correction, you may send us a statement explaining why you believe the information is wrong, and we will keep it in your file. Residents of some states may have additional rights under state law.
