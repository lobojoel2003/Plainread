PLAINREAD - understand hard letters in plain words
===================================================

Plainread runs on your own computer. Your letters are never sent over the internet.

START
  Windows:  double-click plainread.exe
            (if Windows SmartScreen appears, click "More info", then "Run anyway")
  Mac:      right-click "plainread", choose Open, then Open again
            (or in Terminal: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine plainread && ./plainread)
  Linux:    ./plainread

A window with a short message stays open while Plainread runs. Your web browser
opens at http://127.0.0.1:8765 - add your letter there. Close the window to stop.

READING PHOTOS
  To read photos of letters, install the free Tesseract OCR program once:
  Windows:  https://github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract/wiki
  Mac:      brew install tesseract
  Linux:    sudo apt install tesseract-ocr
  Without it you can still paste the text of a letter, or open a PDF.

BETTER SUMMARIES AND FULL TRANSLATION (optional)
  Install Ollama (https://ollama.com), run:  ollama pull gemma3:4b
  then tick "Use local AI" in Plainread.

CHECK YOUR SETUP
  plainread doctor

Plainread helps you understand a letter. It is not legal, medical or money advice.
More help: https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Plainread
